What Precifica 3D does for you

Made by someone who prints and sells. Every number in the calculation is in plain sight, so you understand where the price comes from.

Filament and resin

Two calculation modes: filament by weight, resin by volume (mL). A printer catalog with the parts that wear out — nozzle, build plate, LCD screen, FEP film.

Estimate from an STL file

Upload the .stl file and the app calculates volume, dimensions and estimated weight, with a preview of the part. You adjust the infill and the scale.

Price per marketplace

A true profit margin (not markup) and the minimum listing price on each platform, after commission and fixed fee.

Where every cent goes

A chart shows how much of the price is material, energy, machine depreciation, labor, miscellaneous items and profit.

PDF quote

Generate a clean quote for your customer, with your brand and your terms — without showing your cost or your profit. Requires Google sign-in.

The quote your customer receives

Clean, with your branding, and without showing what the part cost you.

  • Your company's name, contact and city in the header
  • A preview of the part, when you upload the STL file
  • Delivery time, shipping, payment method and validity
  • Only the final price — no cost, no profit

To generate the PDF you sign in with your Google account. Quotes stay saved so you can issue them again anytime.

After the quote, track the order

When the customer replies, you mark it in the app. The rest — monthly summary, customer list and stock — builds itself from there.

Status of each quote

Sent, approved, in production, delivered or declined. The list has a status filter and keeps the date of each change.

Month summary

Quotes issued, approval rate, revenue, actual profit (price charged − part cost), actual margin and average ticket — with arrows to see previous months.

Customers, no sign-up

The list builds itself from the names on your quotes: how many orders each customer placed and how much they've approved. Next time, the app suggests the name as you type.

Filament and resin stock

Enter how much you have of each material (optional). When an order goes into production, the usage comes out of stock on its own, and the actual loss logs failed prints and supports. You turn on the low-stock warning if you want it.

Frequently asked questions

If yours isn't here, just write to us.

Do I need to pay or sign up?

Not to calculate. The app is free and the calculator works without an account. Google sign-in is only needed to sync between devices and generate the PDF quote.

Is my data safe?

Without signing in, everything stays only on your device. When signed in, we store only what you entered, tied to your Google account, and we never sell or share that data. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Are the marketplace fees right?

They're reference fees, on average. Commission changes by category and by seller, so check the platform's official fee calculator before actually listing.

Are the catalog's printer and part prices exact?

They're market estimates so you can start quickly. Adjust them with your own values in Settings — that's what makes the calculation match your reality.

Does stock deduct on its own?

Only for materials where you enter a stock amount, and only on quotes issued as PDF. When you mark the order as "In production", the amount used comes out of stock, along with the loss estimated from the calculation's waste. If you go back in status, it returns. Afterwards you adjust the "actual loss" to what was really lost to failed prints and supports. Materials without a stock amount aren't tracked, and the low-stock warning stays off until you turn it on.

Is the monthly summary profit the real profit?

It's the price you charged minus the cost the app calculated when you issued the quote, summed over the orders approved that month. If you enter the actual cost of miscellaneous items (key rings, clasps, glue…), it replaces the calculation's estimate. The profit doesn't subtract marketplace fees or expenses outside the calculation, such as shipping and rent. It's for tracking each order's margin, not for replacing your accounting.

Can I use it on my phone like an app?

Yes. In Chrome (Android), tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen".

What's the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is the share of the final price that remains as profit: with a 40% margin, $40 of every $100 sold is profit. Markup is how much you add on top of cost, and it yields less profit than it seems. Precifica 3D uses true margin.
