PRECIFICA 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what data Precifica 3D collects, what for, and how you can control it. We've written it plainly — without unnecessary legalese — because the app is a simple tool: a 3D printing cost calculator.

Who is responsible

WDSERTORI SOLUÇÕES TÉCNICAS develops and maintains Precifica 3D.
Contact: wdsertori@gmail.com

Using it without signing in

By default, the app does not collect or send any data. Everything you enter (printers, filaments, quotes) is saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. We never see that data, and it never leaves your phone or computer.

Using it signed in (Google account)

Signing in is optional, and only serves to sync your data between devices. When you sign in with your Google account, we collect:

  • Name, email and profile photo — they come straight from Google (via Firebase Authentication), only to identify your account inside the app.
  • What you enter in the app — printers, filaments and resins (with stock, if you track it), saved quotes (with the status and dates of each) and, if you fill them in, your company details (name, phone, email, city) used on the PDF quote. Your language preference is saved too.

This data is stored on Firebase/Google Cloud (Google's infrastructure), tied only to your account — no other user can access it. The servers may be located outside your country, including the United States, within Google Cloud's global infrastructure.

Your customers' names

When issuing a quote, you can enter the customer's name (optional). That name stays on the quote itself, stored on your device and, if you're signed in, in your account. The app uses these names only to build the "Customers" list and to suggest the name when you type it again — none of this is sent to third parties or used for any other purpose.

Your customers' data is the responsibility of whoever enters it. We recommend entering only what's necessary (the name). To remove a customer, delete their quotes in the app.

What we do NOT do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We don't use tracking or behavior-analytics tools (no Google Analytics or similar).
  • We don't show ads inside the app.

Affiliate links

The "Shopping" section (currently available only in the Portuguese version of the app) leads to external marketplaces using affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. When you click, you leave Precifica 3D and fall under that site's privacy policy, which we don't control. If affiliate links are added to the English version, we will update this page.

PDF quote

The PDF is generated entirely on your own device (it doesn't go through any server of ours). If you're signed in, a copy of the quote data is saved to your account so you can generate it again later.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and edit everything you entered, directly in the app.
  • Delete a specific item (printer, filament, quote) using the delete buttons inside the app.
  • Sign out at any time — your data stays saved in the cloud until you ask for full deletion.
  • Request full deletion of your account and data, by writing to wdsertori@gmail.com. We reply and confirm the deletion within a few business days.

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Union and the United Kingdom under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, portability and objection to processing. To exercise any of them, write to the email above. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

Precifica 3D is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example, a new feature that collects new data), we update this page and the date at the top.

In one sentence: without signing in, nothing leaves your device; when signed in, we keep only what you yourself entered, tied to your Google account, without selling or sharing anything with anyone.
